WebKit's content rule format and to per-domain page rules uses AdGuard SafariConverterLib, a build tool that is not
part of the app.

YOUTUBE RULES

Four rules for youtube.com, youtubekids.com and youtube-nocookie.com were written for Sailfish from the public
field names of YouTube's player response (adPlacements, playerAds, adSlots). They go in PageShield/ads.json.

SCRIPTLETS

The scripts that page rules call are written for Sailfish and are Sailfish's own code:
set-constant, abort-on-property-read, abort-current-inline-script, json-prune, remove-attr, remove-class,
prevent-addEventListener and prevent-setTimeout. They follow the argument conventions filter lists use for
scriptlets of these names. No code from AdGuard Scriptlets, uBlock Origin or Brave is included. The procedural
selector engine and the cookie wall unlock are also Sailfish's own code.
